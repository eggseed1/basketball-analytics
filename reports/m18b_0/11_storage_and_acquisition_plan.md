## Rough sizes (order-of-magnitude)

| Source | Est. raw |
|---|---|
| SportVU one game (~25 Hz) | ~5–15 MB compressed typical in public mirrors |

## Before any download

1. Confirm free disk ≥ 2× estimated raw + normalized
2. Confirm license/ToS
3. Checkpointed resume plan
4. Rate limits
